01 — The Idea

We help the world organize itself.

A20Core is a sovereign intelligence that coordinates a nation's critical infrastructure — energy, industry, production, and the systems that feed and power a people — as one living organism. Not a controller barking orders from the top, but countless intelligences acting together, the way a murmuration moves without a leader.

Our brand carries that idea in everything: warmth over coldness, light over gloom, the human and the intelligent as collaborators, and beauty as a sign of confidence.

“No bird leads the murmuration. Order is not imposed from above — it emerges from below.”

This is a renaissance: the belief that the future can be beautiful, and that humanity, working as one and reaching past the known, is the most powerful instrument ever assembled.

02 — The Logo

One mark, one name, one world.

The zero is the world — a slightly imperfect Earth holding two crossing currents (motion, orchestration, the many). Emblem and wordmark lock together as a single unit: emblem · gold divider · A20Core, with “A20” leading and “Core” as the descriptor, set in Hanken Grotesk.

03 — Color

Fresco light, cosmic depth, gilded accents.

Two grounds — warm Fresco Cream for the hopeful, human surface; deep Cosmos Ink for depth and operator screens. Renaissance Gold is the signature; lapis, emerald, and terracotta are the chromatic voices; coral and teal are expressive accents reserved for art.

06 — Voice

Declarative. Warm. Specific.

We speak with quiet confidence — the register of a sovereign institution that is also profoundly human. Declarative, not hype. Specific numbers, not vague claims. Hopeful, never fearful. We describe a future worth building.

We don't sell control. We offer order — born from many.
We say
  • ✓ “Coordinates the systems a nation depends on.”
  • ✓ “Order emerges; we steward it.”
  • ✓ “A future that is beautiful and humane.”
We avoid
  • ✗ “Total control of everything.”
  • ✗ Hype, fear, buzzwords.
  • ✗ Cold, dystopian, “Big Brother” framing.

08 — In short

Hold the line.

Do
  • ✓ Default to warm cream light; use cosmos for depth.
  • ✓ Let gold be rare and meaningful.
  • ✓ Keep imagery painterly, hopeful, humanist.
  • ✓ Give the logo clear space and one centerline.
Don't
  • ✗ Sterile white, neon, or pure-black gloom.
  • ✗ Gradients of gold→rust, glassmorphism, drop-shadow overload.
  • ✗ Stock photography or cold 3D renders.
  • ✗ Distort, recolor, or crowd the mark.

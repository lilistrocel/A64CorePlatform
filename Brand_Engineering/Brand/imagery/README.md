## Art-direction rules (so future imagery stays on-brand)

- **Painterly, not photographic.** Renaissance fresco / hand-painted finish; visible craft and warmth.
- **Palette:** fresco cream + cosmos ink grounds; Renaissance gold, lapis blue, emerald, terracotta; coral/teal only as expressive accents.
- **Subjects:** humans and intelligence as collaborators; sacred geometry / golden ratio; orbits, constellations, domes, the imperfect Earth.
- **Mood:** hopeful, monumental, humanist. Never dystopian, cold, or sterile.
- **Light:** warm, from within; gold leaf and luminous accents.
